Welcome to Support Triage Studio

A quick 3-step tour so you can understand this environment in under a minute.

1. What this is

This is a realistic customer-support simulation environment for evaluating AI agents. Your goal is to triage tickets, draft responses, and resolve issues efficiently.

2. How to use it

Choose a scenario, reset the episode, then submit actions. The ideal flow is classify ticket -> draft customer reply -> resolve with internal note. Rewards and logs update every step.

3. What to look at

Watch the Episode Guide for running score and progress, and use the Ticket Queue to inspect status changes. You can reopen this tour anytime using the Help Tour button.
